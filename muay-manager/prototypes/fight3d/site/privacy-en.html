Privacy policy

Muay Manager (working title) · last updated 20 November 2026 · ภาษาไทย

You can play without signing up. The game does not ask for your real name, email, phone number or location. This page says what the game keeps, what it is used for, and how you can delete it. The Thai page is the original; this is a translation.

What the game keeps

DataWhere it is keptWhat it is for
A random player id and its keyIn your browser or app, and on the game's server (only a hash of the key)So the game knows whose save is whose
Your save: the fighter's name you chose, looks, stats, in-game money, fight historyOn your device and on the game's serverTo carry on playing, and to move to another device
In-game usage counts (kept inside the save): how often each part of the game is used, such as which training or fights, whether fights are watched to the end or skipped, total play time, how many times and on which days you playedOn your device and on the game's serverTo see which parts of the game are used, to improve it
Ranking data: fighter's name, ranking points, record, looks, last time onlineThe game's server, seen by other playersThe ranking table, challenges, online status
Challenges and answers (fighter, fight plan, time), ranked fights against other players' fighters, cheers in live matches, and sign-ups for player cupsThe game's server, seen by other playersSo both sides and the watchers see the same fight
World chat: fighter's name, what you type, when it was sent, and reports of linesThe game's server (the latest 200 lines of everyone together)So every player can read it, and so the developer can check reported lines
Fight news: fighter's name, result, opponent's name and highlights (a belt won, a move up a league)The game's server (the latest 200 items of everyone together)Shown as a news strip to every player
Error reports: the error message, game version, browser typeThe game's server (the latest 1,000)To fix bugs
Problem reports or suggestions you send from the settings screen, with your fighter's name, game version and screen sizeThe game's serverTo fix problems and improve the game

The server provider sees your IP address, as with any website. The game does not tie IP addresses to player ids.

A fighter's name is text you choose and other players can see. Please don't use your real name or personal details as a fighter's name.

Adverts and purchases (mobile app only)

  • The app shows adverts from Google AdMob, which may collect the device's advertising id, device data and approximate location to choose and measure adverts, under Google's policy. You can turn off personalised adverts in your device's settings.
  • Purchases go through the App Store or Google Play. The game only learns whether something was bought, never your card or bank details.
  • The game's website has no adverts and no purchases.

Sharing

The game does not sell your data and does not give it to anyone, except the providers the game needs to work: the server provider and, in the app, Google AdMob and the app stores.

Deleting your account and data

In the game, go to Settings → Account and data → Delete account. Your player id, save, ranking data, challenges, answers, fight news and world chat lines are removed from the server at once, and cannot be brought back. Data on your device is removed by clearing the site's data in your browser, or by uninstalling the app.

If you can no longer open the game but want your data deleted, contact the developer as below, with your fighter's name.

Children

The game shows a combat sport. It is not made for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children's data.
