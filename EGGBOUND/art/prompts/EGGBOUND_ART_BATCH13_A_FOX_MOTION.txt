1. MASTER STYLE (same as the attached images — every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: identical to the attached images — painterly anime fantasy game art,
soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, fire/particles/sparks, motion
lines, speed lines, text, letters, watermark, frame, second character.
(Fire effects, smears and glows are added by the game in code.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character.

CHARACTER: EXACTLY the fox in the attached images — same face, eye shape,
colors, fur markings, flame-shaped solid-fur tail tip, proportions and
SIZE. Same scale as mon001_front_idle (do not draw it bigger or smaller).

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, character centered, at least 80 px margin.
- Lowest point (paws on the ground) on an invisible ground line at
  y ≈ 870 px. For jumping poses, keep the body at the height it reaches
  but never cut anything off.

VIEWS:
FRONT VIEW = 3/4 front view from the 55° camera, body facing BOTTOM-RIGHT
             (like mon001_front_idle).
BACK VIEW  = 3/4 back view from the same camera, body facing TOP-RIGHT
             (like mon001_back_idle).

POSE PRINCIPLES (very important):
- Push every pose to the extreme — strong anticipation, full extension,
  big follow-through. Clear line of action through the whole body.
- The tail and ears must also act (they lag behind and overshoot).
- Each attack pose must read clearly even as a black silhouette.
