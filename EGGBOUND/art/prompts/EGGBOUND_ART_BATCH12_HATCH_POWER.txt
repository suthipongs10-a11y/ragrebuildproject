0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the VFX artist for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 7 images, ONE IMAGE
PER TURN, in the order below. They are LIGHT LAYERS for the most exciting
moment of the game: hatching an epic or legendary monster. Four crystal
pillars gather power, fire it into the egg, the egg erupts with a roaring
power-up aura, lightning and shafts of light, then explodes into the monster.
The game adds these layers with additive blending, tints them, and animates
them (flicker, scale, rotate, fade).

The mood: a classic shonen-anime power-up — violent, roaring energy that
blasts upward around a body, crackling electricity, sharp light shafts.
Make every effect POWERFUL, dynamic and beautiful. ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not
copy any existing game, anime or film effect or character.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one in the same look.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: high-quality anime game VFX, painterly but clean: hot white cores,
soft luminous falloff, crisp sharp highlights, strong flowing shapes, clear
upward motion. Readable at small size on a phone.
COLOUR: GREYSCALE ONLY — white and greys. NO hue at all. Brightness = light.
BACKGROUND: PURE BLACK #000000 everywhere outside the effect (exactly 0,0,0).
NOT transparent, NOT dark blue, NOT a gradient.
FORBIDDEN: characters, monsters, eggs, objects, faces, ground, scenery,
text, letters, numbers, logos, frames, watermarks, coloured tints.
Every effect fades to black at least 30 px before every canvas edge
(except where stated).

2. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  fx_aura_flame.png
POWER-UP AURA (wraps around an egg or a monster and blasts upward)
Canvas 1024 x 1536 (portrait). Imagine an INVISIBLE rounded body standing in
the lower middle: its bottom at y ≈ 1380, its top at y ≈ 740, about 560 px
wide, centered at x = 512. Draw a roaring aura of energy flames wrapped
around that silhouette: brightest along the body's outline, many sharp
flame tongues bursting UPWARD, the tallest ones above the top of the body
reaching up to y ≈ 100, side flames leaning slightly outward. The INSIDE of
the body area stays mostly dark (the body will be drawn there).
Energetic, spiky, slightly asymmetric — like a power-up aura at full blast.

#02  fx_aura_flame_b.png
SAME AURA, SECOND FRAME (the game alternates #01 and #02 fast to make it
roar). Same size, position, overall shape and brightness as #01, but every
flame tongue in a different position / length — like the next frame of the
same fire.

#03  fx_light_shaft.png
LIGHT SHAFT (many shoot up around the egg, each lasting a split second)
Canvas 256 x 1024 (portrait). One sharp spike of light shooting straight
up: widest and brightest at the BOTTOM center (128, 1000) — about 60 px

#04  fx_energy_beam.png
ENERGY BEAM (each crystal pillar fires one into the egg)
Canvas 1024 x 256. A powerful horizontal beam of energy running from the

twisting energy streaks and small crackles along it, soft glow fading out

#05  fx_vortex_spiral.png
GATHERING VORTEX (light spiralling up around each crystal pillar while it

Canvas 512 x 1024 (portrait). Two or three ribbons of light spiralling

#06  fx_arc_ring.png
ELECTRIC SHEATH (crackling electricity wrapped around the egg)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. Jagged branching lightning arcs forming a rough ring
