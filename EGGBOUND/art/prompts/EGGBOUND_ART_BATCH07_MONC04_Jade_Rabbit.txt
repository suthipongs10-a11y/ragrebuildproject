0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. Generate 12
images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. The images are animated
by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity: every image must
show the exact same character.

The attached mon001_master (a fire fox) is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY:
match its art style, shading, line quality, lighting, camera angle and
level of detail. Do NOT copy the fox's design, colors or body.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER). Show it and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#12 one by one, each time using #01 as the
     character reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Cute-but-cool proportions: slightly big head, expressive eyes, strong pose.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, fire/particles/sparks, motion lines,
text, letters, watermark, frame, second character.
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character (Dota, League of Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, etc.).
Myth source (public domain): Chinese mythology (public domain): the Jade Rabbit who pounds the elixir on the moon.

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 80 px empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of body) sits on an invisible ground
  line at y ≈ 870 px in EVERY image.
- SAME SCALE in every image of this set.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_C04 Jade Rabbit
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Species: the moon's Jade Rabbit — a small white rabbit that pounds herbal medicine. Element LIGHT, role Healer (diligent, gentle, sleepy-cute).
- Body: round fluffy white rabbit standing on hind legs, long ears with pale jade-green inner color.
- Details: holds a jade-green wooden pestle; a small jade mortar bowl strapped on its back; a pale jade sash with a tiny crescent-moon charm.
- Colors: snow white fur, jade green accents, soft silver highlights.
- Face: big soft red-brown eyes, tiny nose, calm smile.
- Size: Common class - body height about 55-60% of the canvas. Simple, readable design.
