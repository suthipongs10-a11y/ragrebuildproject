1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art, premium collectible quality.
Glossy dimensional shading, crisp edges, rich material detail (enamel,
pearl, polished metal, cut gems), clean readable silhouette, no blur.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT with a crisp specular highlight,
soft cool fill from right, a thin bright rim light on the right edge.

FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, pedestal, nest, cracks, glow halos outside

(The game adds aura, sparkles, light rays and cracking in code.)

EGG SHAPE & PIVOT (identical for all five eggs #01–#05):
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 PNG.
- A classic upright egg, slightly narrower at the top, standing upright.
- Egg centered at x = 512, bottom of the egg at y ≈ 870.
- Egg height ≈ 560 px (≈ 55% of the canvas), width ≈ 410 px.
- Same shape, size, position, camera (front view, very slightly from
  above) for #01–#05 so they swap cleanly in the game.
