---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D fantasy monster-collecting RPG.
The attached images show the finished character "Dawnflame Fox":
  - mon001_front_idle.png = FRONT VIEW reference
  - mon001_back_idle.png  = BACK VIEW reference
Your job: draw its WALK CYCLE as sprite sheets that will be sliced
and played as animation frames in the game. The fox must look like the
EXACT same character in every frame (same design, colors, markings,
proportions, line style, lighting). Only the legs, body bob, head and
tail sway change between frames.

Work order (one image per turn):
  1. Generate image #01 (FRONT walk sheet). Show it and wait for "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate image #02 (BACK walk sheet).
  3. If I approve both, generate the optional images #03 and #04 (RUN
     sheets) only if I say "do the run too".
  4. Package into a ZIP (section 6).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. SHEET LAYOUT (very important — the game slices it automatically)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Canvas: 1024 x 1024 px, square, PNG.
- A 2 x 2 GRID of four equal cells, each 512 x 512 px:
      cell A (top-left)     = frame 1
      cell B (top-right)    = frame 2
      cell C (bottom-left)  = frame 3
      cell D (bottom-right) = frame 4
  Reading order: A → B → C → D, then it loops back to A.
- Do NOT draw grid lines, borders, numbers or labels. The grid is
  invisible; just place one fox in the middle of each cell.
- In every cell:
  * the fox is centered horizontally in its cell
  * the whole fox (tail tips and ear tips included) stays inside its
    own cell with at least 24 px empty margin — nothing crosses into
    another cell
  * the paws that touch the ground sit on an invisible ground line at
    ~85% of the cell height (y ≈ 435 px inside each cell)
  * the fox is the SAME SIZE in all four cells (about 80% of the cell
    width from nose to tail tip)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. GLOBAL RULES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT. If impossible, use perfectly flat
  solid magenta #FF00FF everywhere (no gradient, no texture).
- Style: identical to the reference — painterly anime fantasy game
  art, soft dimensional shading, crisp clean edges, no blur.
- Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
- Camera: high angle ~55° looking down (oblique top-down), same as
  the reference images.
- STRICTLY FORBIDDEN: ground, grass, cast shadow, contact shadow, glow,
  aura, fire, particles, sparks, dust, motion lines, speed lines, text,
  numbers, watermark, borders, grid lines, a second character,
  clothing or accessories.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WALK CYCLE — WHAT EACH FRAME SHOWS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
A cute, bouncy quadruped walk (diagonal leg pairs move together, like
a trotting puppy). Clear leg positions so the motion reads at small
size.

frame 1 (cell A) — CONTACT 1
  Front-LEFT paw reaching forward and touching down, back-RIGHT paw
  reaching forward and touching down. The other two legs are pushed
  back. Body at normal height. Tail swings slightly to one side.

frame 2 (cell B) — PASSING 1
  Legs gathered under the body (front legs close together, back legs
  close together), one front paw slightly lifted. Body RAISED a little
  (about 10 px higher than frame 1). Ears perk up. Tail centered.

frame 3 (cell C) — CONTACT 2
  Mirror of frame 1 leg pattern: front-RIGHT paw forward and touching
  down, back-LEFT paw forward and touching down, other legs pushed
  back. Body at normal height. Tail swings slightly to the other side.

frame 4 (cell D) — PASSING 2
  Like frame 2 but the OTHER front paw is slightly lifted. Body raised
  a little. Tail centered.

Keep the head mostly level (small natural bob only), happy relaxed
expression, mouth closed, eyes open.
