---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 6 images,
ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. These images are placed and
animated by code, so EXACT camera angle, scale, centering and pivot
position matter more than creativity. Never add things that the
instructions say code will draw.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one, each time using the
     approved images as reference for style, palette and camera.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Warm, cozy, storybook mood. Rich but not noisy detail.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: cast shadow on the ground, glow halos, aura, particles, sparks, smoke,
motion lines, text, letters, numbers, watermark, frame, characters/creatures
(unless the item IS the creature), people.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy or resemble any existing game, film or anime
asset (Summoners War, Clash of Clans, Pokemon, Studio Ghibli, etc.).
The home is based on the public-domain myth of the giant sea turtles that
carry the islands of the immortals (Chinese myth of Ao / Penglai) and the
world-turtle Kurma (Hindu myth).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAMERA
---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA (identical for every image in this batch):
High 3/4 view, camera elevation about 35 degrees above the horizon, looking
slightly down. Orthographic feel, no strong perspective distortion.
A circle on the ground appears as an ellipse with height/width ratio ≈ 0.55.
The front of every object faces the viewer (toward the bottom of the image).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PALETTE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
SHARED PALETTE / MATERIALS (so all buildings feel like one world):
- Cream / warm-white carved stone with soft moss at the base
- Terracotta-red roofs; special roofs in deep blue or gold
- Bronze and gold trims, small egg-shaped emblems as the home's motif
- Wood: warm honey brown. Fabric: red and cream banners (no writing)
- Architecture = a gentle blend of European storybook castle + Thai/Chinese
  curved tiered eaves and finials. Elegant, not overly ornate.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3b. CHARACTER
---------------------------------------------------------------------
THE TURTLE — "Ancient Cloud Turtle" (เต่าบรรพกาล)
A colossal, gentle, ancient sea turtle that swims through a sea of clouds
and carries the player's home on its shell. Kind and sleepy, cute-but-majestic.
- Skin: mossy jade-olive green with soft golden freckles, slightly wrinkled,
  a few tiny moss patches. Belly side lighter cream-green.
- Shell rim + side: warm bronze-brown, thick, made of large rounded
  marginal scutes; each scute has a faint carved cloud-spiral motif.
- Shell TOP: a flat, gently domed MEADOW (soft green grass, a few tiny
  flowers in cream/pink/yellow, faint hexagonal scute seams showing
  through the grass). This top surface must be EMPTY and OPEN because the
  game places buildings on it by code.
- Head: big round friendly face, large shiny dark eyes with highlights,
  small smile, rosy cheeks, a little sprout of two leaves on top of the head.
