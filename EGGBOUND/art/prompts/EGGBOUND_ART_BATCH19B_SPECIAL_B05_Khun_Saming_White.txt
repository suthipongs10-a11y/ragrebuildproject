0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. Generate 12
images of ONE special monster, ONE IMAGE PER TURN. The images are animated
by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity: every image must show
the exact same character.

This is the rare LIGHT-element cousin of the Saming tiger (attached
monB05_master: a lean indigo-black shadow tiger). Keep the family link —
golden slit eyes, a pale crescent-moon mark on the forehead, one notched
ear — but this one is a WHITE tiger who stands on two legs like a man and
dresses as a legendary war lord (khun) of the old Siamese kingdom.
The most collectible version of the family: grand, noble, unforgettable.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER) and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#12 one by one, using #01 as the reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then deliver the files (section 6).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Detailed and majestic, but clean and readable at small size on a phone.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, halo, light rays, fire/particles,
sparks, motion lines, dripping blood or gore, text, letters, watermark,
frame, second character.
(The game adds all aura, glow and effects in code.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy any existing game/film character, and do not
resemble any existing game, film, anime or novel character (Dota, League of
Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, Digimon, Kung Fu Panda, etc.).

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 80 px empty margin
  on every side (the axe too — nothing may touch the canvas edge).
- The lowest point (feet) sits on an invisible ground line at y ≈ 870 px in
  EVERY image.
- SAME SCALE in every image of this set.
- Size: LEGEND class - body height about 72-76% of the canvas.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — Khun Saming the White (LIGHT, Attacker)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Species: a white tiger spirit standing UPRIGHT ON TWO LEGS like a man:
  broad shoulders, powerful arms, tiger head, digitigrade tiger legs with big
  clawed feet, a long tail.
- Fur: pearl-white fur with soft pale-gold and warm grey tiger stripes; the
  stripe tips on the forearms and tail curl like painted cloud-scrolls
  (painted on the fur, nothing floating away).
- Head: golden-yellow slit-pupil eyes, a pale-gold crescent-moon mark on the
  forehead, one notched ear (the family marks), long white whiskers.
  Expression: noble, fearless, a war lord's calm.
- RED HEADBAND: a long crimson-red cloth band tied around the brow (above the
  crescent mark, which stays visible), knotted at the back with two long tails
  that stream behind the head. The signature — must read instantly.
- Attire of an ancient Siamese war lord (khun), grand and ornate:
  * a fitted white-and-gold war jacket with a high collar and gold-thread
    Thai kranok (flame-scroll) trim,
  * gold lacquered shoulder guards shaped like stylised tiger heads,
  * a gold breastplate medallion and a diagonal crimson sash across the chest,
  * gold arm bands on the upper arms and wide gold cuffs on the wrists,
  * wrapped knee-length trousers in deep royal blue with a gold patterned hem
    (Southeast-Asian wrapped-cloth style), shin guards of gold-trimmed leather,
  * a short layered war skirt of gold-edged crimson panels at the hips.
  Keep ornaments in SOLID shapes (white, gold, crimson, royal blue) with clear
  edges — no thin noisy filigree everywhere.
- BLOOD AXE (ขวานโลหิต): a huge two-handed great axe, as tall as his chest;
  one broad crescent blade of DARK CRIMSON steel with a few blood-red painted
  rune veins, a gold tiger-head fitting where blade meets haft, a long white
  lacquered haft wrapped with red cord and a gold pommel. Heavy and legendary,
  clean steel — NO dripping blood.
- Feel: the noblest and most terrifying lord of the old kingdom — white and
  gold with striking crimson. Majestic, grand, unforgettable.
