0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the item artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG (hand-painted,
top mobile-RPG quality). The game has RUNES: small magic stones a monster
wears in 4 slots. Each rune is drawn by the game from two separate pieces:
  - a FRAME (the amulet case; its outline tells the slot), and
  - a GEM (the round stone in the middle; its colour and emblem tell the set).
The game places any gem in the centre of any frame, adds the grade glow, the
stars and the "+level" text itself. So paint ONLY the pieces, separately.
Generate 14 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

Theme: ancient Siamese-Khmer amulets (like a sacred amulet set in an antique
gold case), mixed with a touch of mythic fantasy. Precious, old, handmade.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (STYLE SAMPLE, a full assembled rune) and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#14 one by one, matching #01 exactly.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then deliver the files (section 6).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly fantasy game item icon, rich hand-painted texture, crisp
clean edges, strong readable silhouette, soft dimensional shading. Readable
when shown small on a phone (about 64 px) — big simple shapes, no fine noise.
VIEW: straight-on FRONT view (no tilt, no perspective), like an icon, with a
gentle bevel so it looks carved and solid.
LIGHT: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from the right, the same in
every image.
BACKGROUND: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF everywhere outside the
object if transparency fails). Nothing else in the picture.
FORBIDDEN: glow, aura, light rays, sparkles, lens flare, particles, smoke,
magic effects, cast shadow on the background, text, letters, numbers, roman
numerals, stars, watermark, frame around the canvas, hands, characters.
(The game adds all glow, sparkle, grade colour, stars and numbers in code.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy any existing game/film character or item, and
do not resemble the runes of Summoners War or any other game (no SW rune
shapes, no SW set symbols).

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, object centred, at least 60 px empty margin.
- Every FRAME (#02–#05) has the SAME outer size and the SAME centre recess
  (see section 2), so the gems fit all of them.
- Every GEM (#06–#13) has the SAME size, shape and cut — only colour and
  emblem change.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE PIECES
---------------------------------------------------------------------
FRAMES (amulet cases) — one per slot:
- Material: antique gold-bronze case with a pale weathered sandstone inner
  band; Thai kranok (flame-scroll) relief along the border; small round
  rivets; aged, slightly worn, precious. NEUTRAL colours only (gold, bronze,
  sandstone) so every gem colour fits.
- Outer size: the frame fills about 86% of the canvas (its widest part).
- CENTRE RECESS: a perfectly ROUND dark cup in the exact centre, diameter
  about 50% of the canvas (512 px), flat dark brown-black stone inside, with a
  thin gold lip around it. Keep the recess EMPTY (no gem in it) and flat dark
  (do NOT make it transparent or pink).
- Keep the four frames identical in material, detail level and lighting —
  only the outline shape changes.

GEMS — one per set:
- A polished round cabochon gem (domed, smooth), diameter about 76% of the
  canvas, with deep colour and soft inner depth (painted, not glowing).
- The set EMBLEM is carved into the gem's face and inlaid with pale gold —
  bold, simple, centred, about 55% of the gem's width. Same stroke weight on
  all eight.
- A thin antique-gold rim around the gem.
