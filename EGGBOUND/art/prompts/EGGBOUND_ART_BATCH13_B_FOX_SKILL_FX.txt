0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the VFX artist for a 2.5D monster RPG with the effect quality of
top mobile monster RPGs. Generate 6 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN. They are
LIGHT LAYERS: the game tints them, adds them with additive blending and
animates them (scale, rotate, stretch, flicker, fade).

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one in the same look.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: high-quality anime game VFX, painterly but clean: hot white cores,
soft luminous falloff, crisp sharp highlights, strong flowing shapes.
Readable at small size on a phone.
COLOUR: GREYSCALE ONLY — white and greys. NO hue at all. Brightness = light.
BACKGROUND: PURE BLACK #000000 everywhere outside the effect (exactly 0,0,0).
NOT transparent, NOT dark blue, NOT a gradient.
FORBIDDEN: characters, animals, faces, eyes, objects, ground, scenery,
text, letters, numbers, logos, frames, watermarks, coloured tints.
Every effect fades to black at least 30 px before every canvas edge.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy effects from any existing game or anime.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  fx_foxfire.png
FOX-FIRE WISP (magic flame orbs that circle the fox and fly at enemies)
Canvas 512 x 768 (portrait). One magical flame wisp: a round glowing
core in the lower part (centre ≈ 256, 520, radius ≈ 90) with an elegant
flickering flame tongue rising from it and curling at the tip like a
teardrop flame, two or three small side licks. Mystical, not destructive.

#02  fx_bite.png
BITE MARK FLASH (the moment the fox's jaws snap shut on a target)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. Two opposing jagged crescent arcs (an upper and a
lower jaw shape) snapping together at the centre, each made of 3–4
sharp fang-like spikes of light, with a bright impact flash where they
meet and a few short sharp sparks flying out.

#03  fx_fire_trail.png
DASH TRAIL (left behind when the fox dashes through enemies)
Canvas 1536 x 512. A long horizontal streak of flame energy, thin and
sharp at the RIGHT end (the front), widening and breaking into wispy
flame tongues toward the LEFT end (the back), with small flying embers
along it. Centred on y = 256.

#04  fx_hit_spark.png
HIT SPARK (used by every monster when a blow lands)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. A sharp comic-style impact star: a hot white core,
6–8 irregular long sharp spikes of different lengths radiating out,
a thin broken shock ring, and small flying shards. Very punchy.

#05  fx_hit_spark_b.png
HIT SPARK, VARIANT (the game alternates #04 and #05 so hits don't repeat)
Same size and centre as #04, different spike arrangement: more spikes,
slightly rotated, with two short curved slash marks crossing the core.

#06  fx_slash_arc.png
SWIPE SMEAR (a quick curved swipe behind claws, tails or weapons)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. One crescent arc of light sweeping from the upper
left around to the lower right (like a fast claw swipe), razor-bright
thin leading edge on the outside, soft feathered trail on the inside,
fading out along its length.
