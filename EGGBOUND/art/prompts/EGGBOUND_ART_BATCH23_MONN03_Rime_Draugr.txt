0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. Generate 12
images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. The images are animated
by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity: every image must
show the exact same character.

The attached mon001_master (a fire fox) is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY:
match its art style, shading, line quality, lighting, camera angle and
level of detail. Do NOT copy the fox's design, colors or body.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER). Show it and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#12 one by one, each time using #01 as the
     character reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Fierce-but-appealing proportions: these monsters come from a legendary forbidden land, so they are cooler, wilder and more imposing than the game's ordinary monsters (never cute-chibi), with expressive eyes and strong poses.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, fire/particles/sparks, motion lines,
text, letters, watermark, frame, second character.
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character (Dota, League of Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, etc.).
Myth source: Norse mythology (public domain): the draugr, a restless warrior who keeps guarding after death.

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 80 px empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of body) sits on an invisible ground
  line at y ≈ 870 px in EVERY image.
- SAME SCALE in every image of this set.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_N03 Rime Draugr
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Species: a knight who entered Niflheim long ago and never came back; the ice now keeps him walking and guarding. Silent, stubborn, sad. Element DARK, role Defender.
- Body: a stocky armoured warrior whose whole body is hidden in old dented iron armour covered in a thick crust of clear ice; a tattered dark cloak frozen stiff.
- Colors: dark iron, clear blue ice crust, a faded midnight-navy cloak, a dull brass trim.
- Head: a closed iron helmet with a narrow visor slit — only darkness behind it (no face, no glowing eyes, no skull).
- Gear: a big round wooden-and-iron shield frozen over, carved with invented (unreadable) rune-like marks; a short heavy sword; an old unlit travel lantern hanging from his belt.
- Must NOT resemble Game of Thrones' wights or White Walkers, nor any undead of Warcraft: no ice-skin, no glowing blue eyes, no rotting flesh, nothing gory.
- NIFLHEIM SET LOOK (shared by all 7 monsters of this forbidden land): each has a few pale frost-fern patterns and small ice crystals growing on its body or gear (painted, NOT glowing), one deep midnight-navy accent colour, and slightly darker, wilder shapes than the game's ordinary monsters — they look dangerous, ancient and mysterious, still beautiful, never gory.
- Size: Rare class - body height about 62-66% of the canvas.
