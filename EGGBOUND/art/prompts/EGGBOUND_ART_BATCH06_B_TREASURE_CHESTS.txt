---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 6 images,
ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. These images are placed and
animated by code, so EXACT camera angle, scale, centering and pivot
position matter more than creativity. Never add things that the
instructions say code will draw.

The chest appears after every won battle. The player taps it, it shakes,
light leaks from the seam, then it bursts open. It must look TEMPTING,
chunky and satisfying — the kind of chest you can't wait to open.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one, each time using the
     approved images as reference for style, palette and camera.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Chunky, slightly stylized proportions (a bit wider and rounder than a real
chest), strong readable shapes at small size on a phone.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: cast shadow on the ground, glow halos, light rays, light coming
out of the chest, aura, particles, sparks, coins or items flying, motion
lines, text, letters, numbers, watermark, frame, characters, people.
(Code adds all light, rays, coins and items in the colour of the loot.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy or resemble any existing game, film or anime
asset (Summoners War, Clash of Clans, Clash Royale, Zelda, Genshin Impact,
Diablo, etc.). The egg-oval emblem is this game's own motif.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAMERA
---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA (identical for every image in this batch):
Front 3/4 view: camera about 25 degrees above, looking straight at the
FRONT of the chest (no turn to the side, the front face is a clean
rectangle facing the viewer). Very mild perspective. The top of the lid and
a thin strip of the chest's top edge are visible.

PIVOT RULES (every image):
- Canvas 1024 x 1024, transparent.
- Chest centered at x = 512. Front-bottom edge of the chest at y = 860.
- Chest body width ≈ 62% of canvas (≈ 640 px), same for both chests.
- The CLOSED, AJAR and OPEN images of the same chest are pixel-aligned:
  the BODY (everything below the lid hinge line) is IDENTICAL in all three;
  only the lid changes.
- Lid hinge line (top edge of the body) at about y = 560.
