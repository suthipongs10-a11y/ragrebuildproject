0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. Generate 16
images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. The images are animated
by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity: every character
image must show the exact same character.

The attached mon001_master (a fire fox) is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY:
match its art style, shading, line quality, lighting, camera angle and
level of detail. Do NOT copy the fox's design, colors or body.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER). Show it and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#16 one by one, each time using #01 as the
     character reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
THIS IS A MAJOR BOSS: imposing, regal, menacing proportions — tall, broad,
heavy and ancient. NOT cute, NOT chibi, but still exactly the same
painterly game style as the reference.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, snowflakes, frost mist,
particles, sparks, light rays, motion lines, text, letters, watermark,
frame, second character. (The game adds the swirling ice aura, the
glow and the snow in code.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character (Dota, League of Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, etc.).
In particular it must NOT resemble:
  - the Lich King of Warcraft (no horned helmet-crown, no skull shoulder
    guards, no runic greatsword, no black plate armor with blue trim),
  - the Night King of Game of Thrones (no bald head with ice horns),
  - the Witch-king / Ringwraiths or Sauron of The Lord of the Rings
    (no empty hooded robe with a floating crown, no spiked black helmet),
  - the White Witch of Narnia, Marvel's Frost Giants, or any ice queen.
Myth source (public domain): Norse mythology — the Hrimthursar (rime
giants) of Niflheim, the primordial realm of ice and mist, and their
king Thrym. Our king is our own creation inspired by that realm.

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 60 px empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of the cape) sits on an invisible ground
  line at y ≈ 900 px in EVERY character image.
- SAME SCALE in every character image of this set.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_Z01 Hrimarch, the Frost Sovereign
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Who: an ancient warrior-king of a lost frozen kingdom, who sat on his
  throne under the glacier for a thousand years until the ice became part
  of him. Silent, cold, patient, terrifying — a mysterious sovereign, never
  a brute. Element WATER (ice). Role: BOSS.
- Size: BOSS class — body height about 76-80% of the canvas INCLUDING the
  crown (top of the crown still at least 60 px from the top edge). Tall,
  broad shoulders, long legs, a heavy cape: a towering silhouette that
  reads clearly on a phone.
- CROWN (his signature): a tall crown of clear glacier ice that GREW out of
  an ancient dark-silver circlet — shaped like a tiered temple spire: a
  tall central spike with smaller stepped spikes around it (7 points in
  all), clear ice with very faint aurora tints inside (pale teal and
  violet, painted, NOT glowing), and one deep sapphire stone set in the
  circlet over the brow. The crown sits ON his head; it is not a helmet.
- FACE: hidden behind a smooth MASK of frosted white ice carved as a calm,
  expressionless, noble human face (closed lips, straight nose, empty
  calm brow). A thin crack runs diagonally across the mask; through the
  crack and the eye holes there is only deep darkness with two small pale
  ice-blue pupils (painted, not glowing). No skull, no visible skin.
- HAIR / COLLAR: long straight frost-white hair falling behind the mask
  onto a thick collar of pale grey-white fur.
- ARMOR: layered plates of ancient dark-blue glacier ice framed in worn
  frost-silver metal, etched with natural frost-fern patterns (like frost
  on a window). Shoulder guards are big angular glacier slabs with sharp
  broken edges — NO skulls, NO horns, NO spikes on the head.
- FROZEN HEART: in the centre of the chest plate, a round window of clear
  ice holds a dark heart-shaped stone sealed inside (it is his secret and
  his weak point). Painted, not glowing.
- CAPE: a long heavy royal cape of deep midnight-navy cloth, its hem
  frozen solid into a fringe of hanging icicles; it reaches the ground.
- WEAPON: a massive GLAIVE (a long polearm with a single curved blade, like
  a Thai ngao / guandao) — a long dark-iron haft wrapped in old silver
  bands, and a long curved blade of clear blue glacier ice with a black
  stone frozen at its base. NOT a sword, NOT a mace, NOT a staff.
- HANDS: armored gauntlets of frost-silver with ice-crystal knuckles.
- COLORS: glacier cyan and clear ice, deep midnight navy, frost silver,
  frost-white fur and hair, one sapphire accent; faint teal-violet aurora
  tints only inside the crown and the glaive blade. Cold, elegant, eerie.
