---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the VFX artist for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 7 images,
ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. These are LIGHT LAYERS that the
game adds on top of the scene with additive blending and tints to any
colour, so they must be pure light on pure black.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one in the same look.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: high-quality anime game VFX, painterly but clean: bright hot-white
core, soft luminous falloff, a few crisp sharp highlights, elegant shapes.
COLOUR: GREYSCALE ONLY — white and greys. NO hue at all (no blue, no gold,
no rainbow). Brightness = intensity of the light.
BACKGROUND: PURE BLACK #000000 everywhere outside the light. NOT transparent,
NOT dark blue, NOT a gradient; black must be exactly 0,0,0 so it disappears
when added.
FORBIDDEN: objects, chests, eggs, characters, ground, scenery, text, letters,
numbers, logos, frames, watermarks, coloured tints, vignettes.
Each effect is centered on its canvas and must NOT touch the canvas edges
(fade to black at least 40 px before every edge).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  fx_rays.png
GOD-RAY FAN (behind an opening chest / hatching egg; code rotates it slowly)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. 16–20 long tapered light rays radiating from the exact
center (512, 512), uneven lengths and widths for a natural look, bright
near the center, fading to black before the edges. Small bright core.

#02  fx_burst.png
EXPLOSION FLASH (the instant the chest bursts open / the egg breaks)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. A hot white starburst at the center: a bright round
core, 8 sharp needle spikes (4 long, 4 short) and a soft round bloom,
plus a faint shock ring around it. Centered at (512, 512).

#03  fx_column.png
LIGHT PILLAR (rises from the chest / altar into the sky)
Canvas 512 x 1024 (portrait). A vertical beam of light: brightest and
widest at the BOTTOM center (256, 980), narrowing and fading toward the
top, soft edges, a few faint vertical streaks inside. Bottom edge glows
like the light comes out of an opening.

#04  fx_ring.png
SHOCKWAVE RING (code scales it up quickly)
Canvas 1024 x 1024. A thin bright circular ring of light (radius ≈ 400 px)
centered at (512, 512), crisp on the outside, soft bloom on the inside,
slightly uneven brightness around the ring. Empty black inside.

#05  fx_sparkle.png
TWINKLE STAR (small sparkles; code scatters many of them)
Canvas 256 x 256. A four-point star sparkle: two long thin spikes
(vertical and horizontal) and two short diagonal ones, tiny hot core,
soft halo. Centered at (128, 128).

#06  fx_rune_circle.png
MAGIC RUNE CIRCLE (lies flat on the floor under the hatching altar; code rotates it)
Canvas 1024 x 1024, seen from DIRECTLY ABOVE as a perfect circle (code
squashes it into the floor perspective).
Two concentric thin rings (outer radius ≈ 470 px, inner ≈ 380 px), a band
of 12 ornate abstract magic glyphs evenly spaced between them (original
glyph shapes — NOT real letters of any alphabet, not readable text),
a six-pointed star made of two triangles inside the inner ring, small
dots at the star tips, and a small circle at the center. Line art made of
light, crisp lines with soft glow.

#07  fx_orb.png
SOFT MAGIC ORB (energy ball, floating motes, egg aura)
Canvas 256 x 256. A round glowing orb: hot white center, smooth bloom
falling off to black, a faint inner swirl. Centered at (128, 128).
