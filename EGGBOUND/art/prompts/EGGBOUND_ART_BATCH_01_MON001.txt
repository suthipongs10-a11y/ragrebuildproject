---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D fantasy monster-collecting RPG.
Generate 17 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the exact order below.
These images will be cut into game sprites and animated by code, so
CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity. Every image of the fox must
look like the exact same character.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 (MASTER). Show it to me and wait for "OK"
     before continuing. If I ask for changes, redo #01 only.
  2. After "OK", generate #02 to #17 one by one. For every image
     after #01, use image #01 as the visual reference for the
     character's design, colors, proportions and line style.
  3. After all 17 images are done, package them into a ZIP
     (see section 6).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GLOBAL RULES (apply to EVERY image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Canvas: 1024 x 1024 px, square, PNG.
- Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT.
  If transparency is not possible, use a perfectly flat solid
  magenta background #FF00FF (no gradient, no texture, no vignette).
- Art style: painterly anime fantasy game art, soft dimensional
  shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges (no blur, no
  depth-of-field). Matches high-quality isometric mobile RPG art.
- Lighting: warm key light from the TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from
  the right. Same lighting in every image.
- Camera: HIGH ANGLE, about 55 degrees looking down (oblique
  top-down, like an isometric RPG). Orthographic feel, no strong
  perspective distortion.
- Framing: one subject only, centered horizontally. Full body inside
  the canvas with at least 80 px empty margin on every side.
  Subject height ~ 60–70% of canvas height.
- Baseline: the paws/feet must touch an invisible ground line at
  ~85% of canvas height (about y = 870 px) in every character image.
- SAME SCALE in every fox image (the fox must not grow or shrink).
- STRICTLY FORBIDDEN in every image:
  ground, floor, grass, cast shadow, contact shadow, glow, aura,
  fire particles, sparks, smoke, motion lines, speed lines, magic
  effects, text, letters, watermark, border, frame, UI, second
  character. (All effects and shadows are added later by code.)

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_001 Dawnflame Fox
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Species: small fire fox monster companion, element FIRE, role
  Striker (fast attacker).
- Personality: cute but brave and energetic.
- Proportions: chibi-ish — big head (~40% of body height), compact
  body, short sturdy legs, large fluffy tail about as big as the body.
- Fur: cream-white base. Warm orange-to-red gradient on the ear tips,
  the four paws and the last third of the tail.
- Tail tip: shaped like a stylized flame, but drawn as SOLID FUR
  shapes (3 curling flame-like fur locks). NOT real fire, no glow.
- Face: large amber eyes with a bright highlight, small dark nose,
  small fang visible when mouth is open.
- Forehead: a small diamond-shaped amber ember gem.
- Ears: large, pointed, with fluffy cream inner fur.
- Chest: a fluffy cream ruff.
- No clothing, no accessories (equipment is drawn separately).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VIEW DEFINITIONS
---------------------------------------------------------------------
FRONT VIEW = 3/4 front view from the high 55° camera. The fox's body
             faces BOTTOM-RIGHT of the canvas. We see its face, chest
             and right side.
BACK VIEW  = 3/4 back view from the same high 55° camera. The fox's
             body faces TOP-RIGHT of the canvas. We see its back, the
             tail, and only part of the side of its face/one eye.
