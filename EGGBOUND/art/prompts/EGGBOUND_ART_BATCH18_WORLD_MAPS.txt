---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the environment artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG
(hand-painted, top mobile-RPG quality). Paint the WORLD MAP of one realm
of an ANCIENT CONTINENT: a living landscape of a forgotten age, seen from
high above, with one landmark site per zone. The game overlays the route,
names, stars and boss icons, so you paint ONLY the land.
Generate 3 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly premium fantasy game world map, rich hand-painted
texture (gouache / oil look), soft warm light from TOP-LEFT, clean
readable shapes. The land feels ANCIENT and legendary: weathered stone,
moss and vines on old ruins, cracked stone-slab roads worn by time,
crumbling walls, half-buried giant statues, old stone bridges, giant
ancient trees. Colour grade slightly aged and warm (a hint of old
parchment in the highlights) but still vivid and inviting, not dark.
Southeast-Asian / Thai-Khmer and Chinese-legend fantasy flavour.

CAMERA: high bird's-eye view looking down at about 60°, north at the
top, like a beautifully painted diorama of a whole region. Terrain has
real depth: hills and mountains cast soft shadows, rivers wind through
valleys, coastlines meet a calm sea. No fisheye, no tilt.

LAND: ONE CONTINUOUS LANDMASS across the image (not floating islands):
plains, forests, rivers, hills, mountains, coast. Each zone is a
LANDMARK SITE painted in miniature on the land (about 12–16% of the
image width), clearly separated from the next by natural terrain
(forest, river, ridge) so each one reads on its own. Faint old dirt
trails or worn stone roads may wind between sites, but they must stay
subtle and must NOT look like a dotted line — the game draws its own
route. Keep the ground right around each site calm (grass, sand, stone
plaza) so the game's name tag below it stays readable.

LANDMARK POSITIONS (centre of each site, % of the 16:9 image, x from the
left, y from the top) — keep them exactly: see each image below.

EDGES: the outer ~5% of the image fades softly into sea mist / low
cloud (no hard frame, no border, no vignette ring).

FORBIDDEN: any text, letters, numbers, map labels, legend, compass rose,
scale bar, grid, dotted route lines, arrows, pins, flags with writing,
banners, UI, frames, borders, torn-paper edges; monsters, people,
characters, animals; glow halos, lens flare, floating particles.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy any existing game/film character, map or
any existing game's UI (including the Summoners War world map).

OUTPUT: 16:9, as large as possible (2048×1152 or more).
