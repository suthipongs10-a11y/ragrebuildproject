---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the environment artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG
(hand-painted, Summoners-War-like quality). Paint the WORLD MAP chart
of one realm: a sea of clouds with floating islands, one island per
zone. The game overlays the route, names, stars and boss icons, so you
paint ONLY the landscape. Generate 3 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly premium fantasy game world map, rich hand-painted
texture, soft warm light from TOP-LEFT, clean readable shapes, cozy and
magical. Southeast-Asian / Thai-Khmer and Chinese-heaven fantasy flavour.

CAMERA: high bird's-eye view looking down at about 50–60°, like a
tabletop diorama of floating islands. No fisheye, no tilt.

WORLD: an endless SEA OF CLOUDS (soft, layered, slightly tinted by the
realm's colour) with glimpses of sky between. Each zone is a FLOATING
ISLAND (rocky underside with hanging roots / waterfalls into the
clouds) whose top shows that zone's landmark in miniature. Islands are
about 16–20% of the image width, clearly separated, with calm cloud
space between them so the game can draw a dotted route.

ISLAND POSITIONS (centre of each island top, % of the 16:9 image,
x from the left, y from the top) — keep them exactly:
  see each image below.

FORBIDDEN: any text, letters, numbers, map labels, compass, route
lines, arrows, pins, flags with writing, UI, frames, borders; monsters,
people, characters; glow halos, lens flare, particles.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy any existing game/film character, map or
any existing game's UI.

OUTPUT: 16:9, as large as possible (2048×1152 or more), no border,
no vignette. Leave the outer 6% of the image as clouds only.
