---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the UI artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG. The game's
home is an ancient giant turtle carrying an island through a sea of
clouds; players hatch monsters from eggs. You paint the textures of the
player's ADVENTURE BOOK — an old, well-loved explorer's journal.
Generate 5 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one in the same style.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly fantasy game art, warm and cozy, rich hand-painted
texture, soft light from TOP-LEFT. Palette: deep oxblood / brown leather,
warm aged parchment, polished gold leaf like the attached chest, small
accents of ruby red.
FORBIDDEN: text, letters, numbers, runes that look like real writing,
logos, watermark, characters, UI buttons.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy any existing game/film character or any
existing game's UI.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  book_page.png — PARCHMENT PAGE (opaque, fills the whole canvas)
Canvas 1024 x 1024, NO transparency. A single page of thick aged
parchment seen straight on: warm cream (#f2e4c2) centre fading to a
light toasted edge, very subtle paper fibres and a few faint water
stains/foxing near the edges only. The CENTRE 80% must stay calm and
light so dark-brown text stays readable on top. No drawings, no lines,
no text on the page.

#02  book_cover.png — LEATHER COVER (opaque, fills the whole canvas)
Canvas 1024 x 1024, NO transparency. Tooled oxblood-brown leather,
seen straight on, with soft wear on the edges, fine pebbled grain and a
blind-embossed (no gold) border pattern of cloud swirls near the edges.
Mostly even so it can sit behind the pages; darker vignette at the
borders.

#03  book_emblem.png — COVER EMBLEM (round medallion)
Canvas 512 x 512, TRANSPARENT background, medallion centered filling
about 85%. A round polished-gold medallion: an egg resting on a small
stylised turtle shell, framed by a ring of tiny cloud swirls, one small
ruby at the bottom. Bold shapes, readable at 56 px.

#04  book_seal.png — WAX SEAL (claim button)
Canvas 512 x 512, TRANSPARENT background, seal centered filling about
85%. A round red wax seal (#b02a1c) with a naturally irregular melted
edge, glossy highlights, the stamped centre slightly sunken and
EMPTY/SMOOTH (the game writes the word "รับ" on it, so no emblem or
letters in the middle — just a smooth plain disc with a thin raised
ring).

#05  book_ribbon.png — BOOKMARK RIBBON
Canvas 128 x 512 (tall), TRANSPARENT background. A satin bookmark
ribbon in deep crimson hanging straight down, filling the canvas width
and height, soft folds and sheen, the bottom end cut in a swallowtail
(V notch). No text, no tassel.

  [ ] #04 seal centre is EMPTY (no symbol, no letters)
  [ ] No text, no logos, no characters anywhere
