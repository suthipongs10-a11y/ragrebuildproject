0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the environment artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG
(hand-painted, top mobile-RPG quality). Paint REALM 4 of the game's
ancient continent: NIFLHEIM, a legendary forbidden land of eternal ice
and mist at the edge of the world. Old tales say that whoever goes in
almost never comes back. It must feel MYSTERIOUS, COLD, SILENT and
BEAUTIFUL-BUT-DANGEROUS — never gory, never horror.
Generate 5 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN: #01 the world map, then #02–#05
the four battle arenas.

Myth source (public domain): Norse mythology — Niflheim, the primordial
realm of ice and mist, home of the rime giants. ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not
copy any existing game/film character, map, scene or any existing game's
UI (including Summoners War); it must not look like Game of Thrones'
lands beyond the Wall or Warcraft's Northrend / Icecrown.

Shared colour grade for all 5: cold glacier blues and silver-whites,
deep midnight navy in the shadows, dark pine greens, old grey stone;
faint aurora tints (pale teal and violet) only in the sky and inside
thick ice. Light from TOP-LEFT, pale and silvery. Never dull-grey: it is
cold but rich and luminous.
