0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the environment artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG
(hand-painted, Summoners-War-like quality). Each battle stage needs one
painted ARENA BACKGROUND: the ground the monsters fight on plus the
scenery around it. The game draws the monsters, health bars, skill
effects, lamps' light, falling leaves/snow and colour grading on top,
so you paint ONLY the place itself, fully lit, in calm daylight/mood.
Generate 14 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (the MASTER LAYOUT) and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#14 one by one, each reusing the EXACT
     camera, arena size and arena position of #01.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER LAYOUT (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly premium fantasy game art, rich hand-painted texture,
soft light from TOP-LEFT, saturated but not neon, clean readable shapes.
Southeast-Asian / Thai-Khmer and Chinese-heaven fantasy flavour.

CAMERA: high three-quarter view looking DOWN at the ground at about
35–40° (NOT straight top-down, NOT a side view): a perfect circle on
the ground looks like an oval 1.67 times wider than tall. Orthographic
feel, no fisheye, no strong perspective convergence. The top edge of
the image is the back of the scene; the bottom edge is nearest.
The image is exactly what the player sees during a battle (the game
draws the monsters on the arena), so the arena is BIG in the frame.

ARENA (identical position in all 14, fractions of the 16:9 image):
- A flat round fighting area (plaza / dais / clearing) seen as a wide
  OVAL: width = 51% of the image width, height = 54% of the image
  height, centre at 50% across and 53% down (so its top edge sits at
  about 26% down and its bottom edge at about 80% down).
- The arena surface is FLAT, EVEN and EMPTY: no props, puddles of
  clutter, tall grass, holes or strong patterns that fight the monsters.
  A subtle inlay ring or a small centre emblem is fine.
- Keep the whole arena fully inside the image with open ground around it.

SCENERY ZONES (so the game can stand monsters in front of it):
- BACK band (top 24% of the image, behind the arena): the landmark of
  the stage — tree trunks, walls, gates, towers, cliffs — standing just
  behind the arena's back edge. Show their LOWER parts clearly (bases,
  doors, roots, wall faces); their tops may run off the top edge.
- SIDES (left/right 20% of the image, beside the arena): medium props
  (trees, rocks, lamp posts, pillars, houses, statues).
- FRONT band (bottom 16% of the image, in front of the arena): ONLY
  low/flat things (grass, water, flowers, low rocks, low railing).
  Nothing taller than about 6% of the image height there — monsters
  stand just above it and must not look hidden behind tall objects.

FORBIDDEN (the game draws these itself — they would double up):
monsters, people, animals, characters, light rays / god rays, lens
flare, sparkles, floating particles, falling leaves/snow/petals in the
air, drifting mist, fog banks, smoke, magic circles, UI, text, letters,
numbers, logos, watermark. (Lit lanterns, braziers with flames, glowing
mushrooms/crystals and glowing lava ARE fine — keep their glow soft and
close to the object.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy any existing game/film character, map,
scene or any existing game's UI.

OUTPUT: 16:9, as large as possible (2048×1152 or more), no border,
no vignette, no frame. Keep the arena floor fairly calm and evenly lit
so the monsters stay readable on it.
