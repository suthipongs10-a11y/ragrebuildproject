0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a premium 2.5D turn-based monster RPG
(hand-painted mobile-RPG quality, on par with the best monster-collecting
games). Generate 12 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. The
images are animated by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than variety:
every image must show the exact same character.

The attached mon001_master (a fire fox) is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match
its painting style, shading, line quality, lighting and camera angle. Do
NOT copy the fox's design, colors, body or its cute proportions.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER). Show it and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#12 one by one, each time using #01 as the
     character reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly fantasy game art, rich hand-painted texture, soft
dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
THIS LEGENDARY IS NOT CUTE: mature, regal, intimidating proportions
(normal-sized head, powerful body), a calm and cold, menacing gaze —
mysterious rather than angry. Think "ancient sovereign beast".
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right,
deep shadows for drama.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT, or one flat solid #FF00FF (magenta) with
no gradient. Do not use magenta/pink anywhere on the character.
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow halo, aura, real fire, smoke,
particles, sparks, motion lines, text, letters, watermark, frame, a
second character. (The game adds all fire and aura effects itself.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character or creature. Myth source (public domain): the Greek
Chimera (a fusion of lion, goat and serpent), reimagined with ONE head.

Technical rules:
- Square canvas (1:1), subject centered, at least 8% empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of body) on an invisible ground line
  at about 85% of the image height in EVERY image.
- SAME SCALE in every image of this set.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_S02 Chimera Rex, the Ember Sovereign
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- ONE head only — a lion's head. NO goat head, NO snake head anywhere.
  The goat lives on as two long, swept-back obsidian-black ram/goat
  horns that rise from the brow like a crown. The serpent lives on as
  the tail: long, covered in dark scales, ending in a curved bronze
  blade (no head, no mouth).
- Mane: thick, heavy, black fading to deep crimson at the tips, shaped
  like smoke frozen in place (solid hair, no fire).
- Eyes: molten ember-gold, narrow and calm.
- A thin gold circlet between the horns with one deep-indigo star gem.
- Thin ember-orange cracks painted only in the seams between armor plates
  (painted color, NOT glowing light).
- Palette: obsidian black, deep crimson, burnished gold; accent of
  deep-indigo night sky. Little bright color — dark and regal.
- STARBORN set look (shared by all 10 of this set): a few small painted
  star-points and thin gold constellation lines worked into the armor and
  markings (painted, not glowing), plus the deep-indigo accent.
- Size: STARBORN LEGEND class — body height about 72–77% of the canvas.
  The most awe-inspiring design in the game, still clear on a phone.

BODY — "Winged Sovereign"
- A colossal lion-bodied beast standing on four legs, built like a king
  of predators: broad chest, heavy shoulders, dark claws with bronze
  claw-caps. Grand and majestic — the kind of monster players dream of
  collecting.
- Black-bronze ornate armor: shoulder plates, a chest plate engraved with
  gold constellation lines, crimson cloth tabards hanging from the belly.
- Two large dragon-like wings: dark outer membranes, the INNER side a
  deep-indigo night sky with small painted stars; slightly tattered
  edges. The wings make the silhouette huge and regal.
- The scaled serpent tail curls behind and rises over the body, its blade
  catching the light.
