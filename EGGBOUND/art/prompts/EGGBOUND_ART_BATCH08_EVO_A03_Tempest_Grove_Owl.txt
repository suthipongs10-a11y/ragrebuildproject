0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. This monster
EVOLVES: when the player raises it enough, it transforms on screen into a
grander adult form. Generate 11 images of the EVOLVED form, ONE IMAGE PER
TURN. The images are animated by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than
creativity: every image must show the exact same evolved character.

The attached monA03_master is the BASE FORM of this same monster. The evolved
form must keep its identity at a glance: same color palette, same signature
markings, same eyes and face style, same species — but bigger, more mature,
more ornate and more powerful. A player must instantly say "it grew up!".

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (EVOLVED MASTER) and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#11 one by one, using #01 as the reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image) — same as the base form
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Evolved forms are more detailed and majestic than base forms, but stay clean
and readable at small size on a phone.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, fire/particles/sparks, motion lines,
text, letters, watermark, frame, second character.
(The game adds all aura, glow and effects in code.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character (Dota, League of Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, Digimon, etc.).

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 80 px empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of body) sits on an invisible ground
  line at y ≈ 870 px in EVERY image.
- SAME SCALE in every image of this set.
- Size: EVOLVED class - body height about 70-75% of the canvas (clearly bigger
  than the base form, which was about 55-60%).
