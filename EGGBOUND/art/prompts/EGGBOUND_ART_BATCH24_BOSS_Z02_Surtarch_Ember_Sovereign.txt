0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG. Generate 16
images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. The images are animated
by code, so CONSISTENCY matters more than creativity: every character
image must show the exact same character.

The attached mon001_master (a fire fox) is a STYLE REFERENCE ONLY:
match its art style, shading, line quality, lighting, camera angle and
level of detail. Do NOT copy the fox's design, colors or body.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (MASTER). Show it and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#16 one by one, each time using #01 as the
     character reference.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP (section 6).

1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
THIS IS A MAJOR BOSS: an evil lord knight — towering, broad, heavy, ancient
and menacing. NOT cute, NOT chibi, but still exactly the same painterly
game style as the reference.
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Camera: HIGH ANGLE about 55 degrees looking down, orthographic feel.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: ground, cast shadow, glow, aura, flames, fire, smoke, embers or
sparks in the air, particles, light rays, motion lines, text, letters,
watermark, frame, second character. (The game adds the fire aura, the
glow and the floating embers in code.)

COLOR RULE (very important — the game recolors him for other elements):
  - EMBER parts = bright, saturated red-orange (like lava, #FF3A1A to
    #FF8A2A): ONLY the two eyes, the lava cracks along the armor edges and
    trim lines, the gems of the crown, and the molten core of the sword.
    Painted bright, but with NO glow halo around them.
  - EVERYTHING ELSE must be LOW-saturation: blackened iron, charcoal,
    dark obsidian grey, dull tarnished bronze. No other red, orange, pink
    or purple anywhere (the cape is charcoal-black, not red).

ORIGINAL DESIGN - do not copy or resemble any existing game, film, anime
or novel character (Dota, League of Legends, Summoners War, Pokemon, etc.).
In particular it must NOT resemble:
  - Sauron of The Lord of the Rings (NO tall black helmet whose spikes
    rise up as a crown, NO huge flanged mace, NO black armor covered in
    spikes, NO ring),
  - the Lich King of Warcraft (no horned helmet-crown, no skull pauldrons),
  - Marvel's Surtur or the God of War fire giant (no horned demon head,
    no giant molten skeleton body, no crown of horns),
  - any Dark Souls / Elden Ring boss (not a lord of cinder, not Gwyn).
Myth source (public domain): Norse mythology — Surtr, the lord of
Muspelheim, the primordial realm of fire, who carries a flaming sword.
Our knight is our own creation inspired by that realm.

Technical rules:
- Canvas 1024 x 1024 px PNG, subject centered, at least 60 px empty margin.
- The lowest point (feet / bottom of the cape) sits on an invisible ground
  line at y ≈ 900 px in EVERY character image.
- SAME SCALE in every character image of this set.

2. CHARACTER SHEET — MON_Z02 Surtarch, the Ember Sovereign
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Who: the last king of a burned-out kingdom, who swore to keep fighting
  after his own death; the fire of his oath still burns inside the armor.
  Proud, cruel, unstoppable — a dark lord knight, never a beast.
  Element FIRE. Role: BOSS.
- Size: BOSS class — body height about 76-80% of the canvas INCLUDING the
  crown (top of the crown still at least 60 px from the top edge). Very
  tall, wide armored shoulders, long cape: a towering silhouette that
  reads clearly on a phone.
- HELM: a closed great-helm of blackened iron whose face plate is forged
  as the stern, expressionless face of a dead king (straight nose, closed
  lips, heavy brow, a short iron-forged beard). Through the two eye holes:
  two burning EMBER-red eyes (the brightest point of the image). No visible
  skin, no skull. The helm itself is smooth and rounded — NO spikes rising
  from it.
- CROWN (his signature): an ANCIENT separate crown worn ON TOP of the helm —
  a wide circlet of dull tarnished bronze-gold, dented and scorched, with
  seven short broad points, two of them broken off; old engraved
  knotwork; three cracked oval gems in EMBER red set in the front. It
  looks a thousand years old and cursed, not shiny.
- ARMOR: heavy layered plate of black obsidian-like iron, smooth broad
  plates (not spiky), with thin LAVA CRACKS running along the plate edges
  and trim lines like molten seams (EMBER color). Big rounded pauldrons
  with three stacked layers; an engraved round seal on the chest showing a
  stylized burning sword. Armored gauntlets and sabatons in the same
  black iron.
- CAPE: a long, heavy, tattered cape of charcoal-black cloth, its torn hem
  scorched to dark grey (NOT burning, NOT red). It reaches the ground.
- WEAPON: a huge TWO-HANDED GREATSWORD of black volcanic glass: a broad,
  slightly jagged blade with a deep central crack running down its length
  that shows a molten EMBER core; a heavy black-iron crossguard shaped like
  two downturned blades; a long grip wrapped in old dark leather; a round
  pommel. NOT a mace, NOT an axe, NOT a hammer, NOT a flail.
- COLORS: black iron and obsidian, charcoal, dull bronze-gold crown, and
  EMBER red-orange ONLY on eyes / armor cracks / crown gems / sword core.
