---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the environment artist for a premium 2.5D fantasy monster RPG
(hand-painted, Summoners-War-like quality). On the monster collection
screen every monster stands full-body on a round display PEDESTAL. You
paint 6 pedestals, one per element. The game adds all lights, auras and
the monster itself on top, so you paint ONLY the stone object.
Generate 6 images, ONE IMAGE PER TURN.

Work order:
  1. Generate #01 (the MASTER SHAPE) and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate #02–#06 one by one. Each one must reuse the
     EXACT silhouette, proportions, size, position and camera angle of
     #01 — only the materials, colours and engraved motifs change.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly premium fantasy game art, rich hand-painted texture,
crisp clean edges, soft light from TOP-LEFT, like the attached hatching
altar (white marble + polished gold). Ornate but readable when small.

SHAPE (identical in all 6):
- A ROUND two-tier dais: a main round tier, and below it a slightly
  wider, lower round step. Low and wide, perfectly symmetrical.
  No stairs, no pillars, no spires, no arches, nothing tall.
- Camera slightly above (about 20–25° down), front view, centred: the
  flat top surface reads as a wide OVAL about 4 times wider than tall.

COMPOSITION (fractions of the image, 16:9 landscape):
- Centred horizontally. The top oval's width = about 60% of the image
  width; its centre at about 42% of the image height.
- Side of the main tier: about 12% of the image height tall, decorated
  with a band of engravings and small gems set in metal.
- Lower step: about 6% of the image height tall, a little wider.
- Rim ornaments may rise at most 4% of the image height above the top
  oval. Keep at least 8% empty margin on every side.

TOP SURFACE: flat, polished, EMPTY. An inlaid ring pattern near the rim
and a calm, plain round area in the middle (the monster stands there).
NOTHING stands on it: no statue, egg, crystal, candle, plant or weapon.

FORBIDDEN (the game draws these itself — they would double up):
glow halo in the air, light rays, lens flare, sparkles, particles,
mist, smoke, fire or water spilling out, magic circles floating above,
cast shadow on the ground, reflections, floor, horizon, scenery.
Any glow must stay INSIDE the stone (glowing veins, runes, gems).

BACKGROUND: one flat pure magenta #FF00FF everywhere around the object
(no gradient, no vignette, no floor, no shadow) so it can be cut out.
Do not use magenta or pink anywhere on the pedestal itself.

Also FORBIDDEN: text, letters, numbers, writing-like runes, logos,
watermark, characters, monsters, UI.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy any existing game/film character, prop
or any existing game's UI.

2. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  ped_light.png — LIGHT element · MASTER SHAPE
White veined marble with polished gold filigree trim. Top: a gold
sunburst inlay ring near the rim. Side band: engraved sun discs and
small clear diamond / pale topaz studs in gold settings. Warm, holy,
royal. (This image sets the shape for all the others.)

#02  ped_fire.png — FIRE element
Dark volcanic basalt with thin molten-orange lava veins glowing INSIDE
the cracks (the glow stays in the cracks). Bronze-gold trim with
flame-curl engravings, small ruby studs. Hot, powerful.

#03  ped_water.png — WATER element
Pearl-white marble with aquamarine / teal inlays, engraved wave scrolls
around the side band, small seashell motifs at intervals, silver-gold
trim, sapphire studs. Clean, cool, elegant.

#04  ped_wind.png — WIND element
Pale jade stone with carved leaf-and-swirl patterns (like a breeze),
green-gold trim, small emerald studs. Tiny carved leaves only — no real
plants sticking up. Fresh, light, airy.

#05  ped_earth.png — EARTH element
Warm sandstone / terracotta with bold carved block patterns, bronze
trim, amber / topaz studs, a little soft moss in the deepest crevices.
Solid, ancient, sturdy.

#06  ped_dark.png — DARK element
Black obsidian with amethyst inlays, silver-violet trim, engraved
crescent moons and small stars, faint violet rune-like lines glowing
INSIDE the stone (abstract shapes, not real letters). Mysterious, noble.

  [ ] Top oval about 4× wider than tall, flat, EMPTY, calm in the middle

      dark = black+violet
  [ ] No text, logos, characters or monsters anywhere
