0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the VFX artist for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 12 images,
ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. They are LIGHT LAYERS for the
monsters' ULTIMATE skills: the game adds them over the battle with additive
blending, tints them to the element colour, and animates them (scale,
rotate, slide, fade). They must be pure light on pure black.

These ultimates are the highlight of the game: the camera zooms in on the
monster, it strikes a pose, and the skill explodes across the screen. Make
every effect POWERFUL, dynamic and beautiful — anime-game climax quality.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one in the same look.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: high-quality anime game VFX, painterly but clean: hot white cores,
soft luminous falloff, crisp sharp highlights, strong flowing shapes and
clear motion direction. Readable at small size on a phone.
COLOUR: GREYSCALE ONLY — white and greys. NO hue at all. Brightness = light.
BACKGROUND: PURE BLACK #000000 everywhere outside the effect (exactly 0,0,0).
NOT transparent, NOT dark blue, NOT a gradient.
FORBIDDEN: characters, monsters, creatures with faces, objects, ground,
scenery, text, letters, numbers, logos, frames, watermarks, coloured tints.
(#02 and #04 are shapes made of light: no eyes, no faces, no realistic detail.)
Every effect fades to black at least 40 px before every canvas edge.
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy effects from any existing game or anime.
