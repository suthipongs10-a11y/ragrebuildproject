---------------------------------------------------------------------
0. YOUR ROLE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You are the art producer for a 2.5D monster RPG. Generate 5 images,
ONE IMAGE PER TURN, in the order below. These images are placed and
animated by code, so EXACT camera angle, scale, centering and pivot
position matter more than creativity. Never add things that the
instructions say code will draw.

This is the most important scene of the game: the player hatches every
monster here. It must feel SACRED, GRAND and MAGICAL — a legendary altar,
not a garden decoration.

Work order:
  1. Generate image #01 and wait for my "OK".
  2. After "OK", generate the rest one by one, each time using the
     approved images as reference for style, palette and camera.
  3. Run the QUALITY CHECK, then package the ZIP.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MASTER STYLE (applies to every image)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
STYLE: painterly anime fantasy game art for a 2.5D turn-based monster RPG.
Soft dimensional shading, clean readable silhouette, crisp edges, no blur.
Majestic, mystical, awe-inspiring, but still warm and inviting.
Rich ornament with a clear hierarchy (big shapes first, detail second).
Lighting: warm key light from TOP-LEFT, soft cool fill from right.
Background: FULLY TRANSPARENT (or flat solid #FF00FF if transparency fails).
FORBIDDEN: cast shadow on the ground, glow halos, aura, particles, sparks,
smoke, light beams, motion lines, text, letters, numbers, watermark, frame,
characters, creatures, people, an egg on the altar.
(Code adds all glow, beams, sparkles, the egg and the colour of the magic.)
ORIGINAL DESIGN — do not copy or resemble any existing game, film or anime
asset (Summoners War, Genshin Impact, Pokemon, Final Fantasy, etc.).
Theme: a cosmic-egg sanctuary — the world was born from a cosmic egg
(public-domain creation myths: Hindu Hiranyagarbha, Chinese Pangu egg,
Orphic world-egg). Motifs: egg ovals, star points, concentric rings,
sun-ray fans, cloud spirals. A blend of Thai/Chinese temple ornament and
ancient European stone carving, like the turtle home buildings.

---------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CAMERA
---------------------------------------------------------------------
CAMERA (identical for every image in this batch except the noted items):
3/4 view, camera elevation about 22 degrees above the horizon (LOWER than
the turtle home — this is a close-up ceremony view). Orthographic feel.
A circle on the ground appears as an ellipse with height/width ratio ≈ 0.38.
The front of every object faces the viewer (toward the bottom of the image).

---------------------------------------------------------------------
3. PALETTE
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ivory / pale moon-white carved stone, very clean, with faint blue-grey
  shadows (NOT mossy — this place is kept pure)
- Polished GOLD trims, inlays and filigree (warm, bright, the main accent)
- Deep night-blue lapis inlay as a secondary accent in small amounts
- All "magic" parts (crystals, gems, rune grooves) are COLOURLESS:
  clear white crystal / white-silver, so the game can tint them to any
  colour. Do NOT paint them blue, gold, green or any hue.

4. IMAGES (one per turn)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
#01  hatch_altar.png
THE HATCHING ALTAR — MASTER IMAGE
Canvas 1024 x 1024, transparent.
A grand round altar of THREE stacked circular tiers, each smaller than the
one below (like a wedding cake of carved stone):
- Bottom tier: wide round stone base with three short steps in front,
  carved panels between gold pilasters, a large clear-white diamond-shaped
  crystal gem set in the front center inside a gold sun-ray mount, and two
  smaller gems left and right.
- Middle tier: a ring of carved egg-oval medallions and gold filigree.
- Top tier: a shallow, perfectly FLAT round dish with a raised gold rim and
  a white-crystal inlaid ring groove on its surface. The dish is EMPTY
  (the game places the egg on it).
- Four tall gold-capped corner finials / small spires rising from the
  bottom tier at the back-left, back-right, front-left, front-right edges
  (the front ones must NOT cover the top dish).
Pivot rules:
- Altar centered at x = 512. Front-most edge of the bottom tier at y = 900.
- Bottom tier ellipse width ≈ 74% of canvas (≈ 760 px).
- Top dish: ellipse about 300 px wide, centered at (512, 540).
  Nothing may overlap the space ABOVE the dish (the egg sits there).
- Highest finial tip no higher than y = 300.

#02  hatch_altar_glow.png
GLOW MASK for #01 (used by code for the colour-changing light)
Canvas 1024 x 1024, background PURE BLACK #000000 (not transparent).
EXACT same altar position and size as #01, pixel-aligned.
Only the parts that should LIGHT UP are painted, in pure WHITE / light
grey with soft falloff: the three gems, the crystal ring groove on the top
dish, thin carved channels/lines between the tiers, the inner faces of the
egg-oval medallions. EVERYTHING ELSE is pure black (stone, gold, outline).
Think of it as "what glows when the altar is powered".

#03  hatch_crystal.png
FLOATING CRYSTAL OBELISK (code makes 4 of them float around the altar)
Canvas 512 x 512, transparent.
A single tall elongated hexagonal crystal, pointed at both ends, clear
WHITE / ice-silver with internal facets and refraction (colourless — the
game tints it). A thin gold band with a small egg emblem wraps its middle.
- Crystal centered at x = 256, upper tip at about y = 50, lower tip at
  about y = 470, widest part ≈ 150 px.
- It floats: no base, no ground, no glow.

#04  hatch_crystal_stand.png
CRYSTAL STAND (the crystal hovers above it)
Canvas 512 x 512, transparent.
A short round pedestal matching the altar (ivory stone + gold rim), with a
small concave crystal-white "socket" disc on top (EMPTY, nothing standing on it).
- Centered at x = 256, front-most edge of its base at y = 470.
- Base ellipse width ≈ 50% of canvas (≈ 256 px). Top disc at about y = 330.

#05  hatch_floor.png
CEREMONY FLOOR (lies under the altar)
Canvas 1536 x 1024, transparent.
A large round floor platform of polished ivory stone tiles with gold seams,
seen with the same camera (ellipse ratio ≈ 0.38): concentric rings of
tiles, an outer gold ring with eight small egg-oval inlays, and an engraved
groove ring (colourless white-silver) where runes would be.
- Ellipse centered at (768, 560), width ≈ 1440 px, height ≈ 547 px.
- Center area (where the altar stands) plain tiles, nothing drawn on top.
- No altar, no crystals, no objects on the floor.
